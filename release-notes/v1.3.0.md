### What's New in This Version  `(1180 / 4000)`
```
Ambagan 1.3 is our biggest update yet — faster, smarter, and built for the way your barkada actually spends.

WHAT'S NEW

• Sign in with Google or Apple — one tap, no password to remember.
• Recurring expenses (Pro) — set rent, subscriptions, and regulars to post themselves on schedule.
• Scan a receipt — snap it and let Ambagan fill in the details for you.
• Travel currencies (Pro) — log expenses in the local currency (PHP, JPY, SGD, and more) on your trips abroad.
• Expense categories — organize spending at a glance with clean category icons.
• A redesigned Add Expense — one simple flow to choose who paid and how it's split, with drafts so you can finish later.

IMPROVEMENTS

• More reliable offline sync — log on the trail or out on the water, and everything syncs cleanly when you're back online.
• A faster, more native feel on the latest iOS.
• Sharper receipts — pinch to zoom and pan on proof of payment.
• CSV export now includes settlement details.
• Search the Help Center FAQ and jump straight to the answer you need.

FIXES

• Fixed a rare hang when switching between accounts.
• Settlement balances now update correctly right after you edit or delete an expense.

Salamat for splitting with Ambagan! Have feedback? Reach us in the app — we read everything.
```

### Description  `(~1730 / 4000)` _(stable)_
```
Ambagan is a group expense splitting app built for friends, families, and barkada. Log shared expenses on the go, split bills across multiple payers, track who paid what, and settle up — without the awkward math.

WHY AMBAGAN

• Split any way you want — equally, by percentage, or with fully custom amounts. Multiple people chipped in for one expense? Ambagan breaks down each person's share automatically.
• Always know where you stand — your net balance across every group sits right on the home screen. Tap any friend to see exactly what you owe each other.
• Settle up with proof — request, approve, or reject settlements with proof-of-payment photos, and bulk-settle across all your groups at once.
• Works offline — the moment you need to record something is exactly the moment you have no signal. Log it right there; Ambagan saves it locally and syncs the second you reconnect.
• Join in seconds — share a group invite link or QR code and friends just scan to join.

NEW WAYS TO LOG
• Scan a receipt and let Ambagan fill in the details.
• Set recurring expenses that post themselves (Pro).
• Log in the local currency when you travel (Pro).
• Sign in fast with Google or Apple.

FREE & PRO
Ambagan is free to download. Free users can log up to 5 expenses a day. Pro removes the cap and unlocks the full feature set — multi-currency, recurring expenses, drafts, and spending analytics — available monthly, yearly, or as a 2-week plan for short trips.

Whether it's a trip, a household, or a regular night out — split it fairly, settle it cleanly.

Learn more at ambagan.ph
```
